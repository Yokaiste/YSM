[h1]💡 Good To Know[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Activate the mod[/b] in WARNO's Mod Center after subscribing
[*]Use only the units you need if you want performance to remain a real concept
[*]Works best for [b]Skirmish[/b] and [b]Multiplayer[/b] sandbox play
[*]Expect conflicts with most [b]gameplay[/b] and [b]UI[/b] mods, because YSM changes basically everything it can reach
[*][b]Map mods are usually fine[/b], because even YSM occasionally respects property boundaries
[/list]
