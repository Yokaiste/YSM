Yokaiste's Sandbox Mod 2.8 — Break the limits. Build the battle you want.

[b]WARNO gives you an army. YSM gives you the entire toy box.[/b]

Field absurdly large divisions, unleash Zombie Horde, launch nukes, or keep unit availability more restrained with Balanced variants. Expanded decks, match settings, and interface layouts give you room to experiment.

[b]Made for Skirmish and Multiplayer sandbox battles.[/b] The figures below describe the standard YSM setup; custom builds can change them.

[h1]🧰 YSM × ANY MOD — Through Yuri's WARNO Toolkit[/h1]

[b]Bring YSM's sandbox to a mod you already love.[/b]

[url=https://github.com/dary1337/yuri-warno-toolkit][b]Yuri's WARNO Toolkit (YWT)[/b][/url] lets you pick a Workshop mod, add YSM, and generate one combined build. Choose the features you want, or add [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3387658237][b]WARNO Tactical Overhaul (WTO)[/b][/url] for its balance changes.

[h2]🚀 Generate a Combination[/h2]
[olist]
[*][url=https://github.com/dary1337/yuri-warno-toolkit/releases/latest][b]Download YWT[/b][/url] and open the toolkit
[*]Subscribe to the base mod in the Steam Workshop
[*]Open the [b]Mod Builder[/b] and select that mod
[*]Choose YSM, optionally add WTO, review the feature choices, and generate your local build
[/olist]

[h2]🃏 Also Inside YWT[/h2]

[h1]🛠️ Built With YMB[/h1]
YSM is built with [url=https://github.com/Yokaiste/YMB][b]Yokaiste's Mod Builder (YMB)[/b][/url]. It keeps a mod's authored changes separate from WARNO's generated files, making large projects easier to maintain through game updates.

[list]
[*]For mod authors: structural patches, bulk rules, shared settings, generation scripts, and automation for tools and AI agents
[*]Build previews and conflict reports help you inspect changes; saved originals support recovery
[*]YSM's source shows these tools working together across divisions, units, localisation, and interface features
[/list]
