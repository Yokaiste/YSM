Yokaiste's Sandbox Mod 2.8

[b]Break the limits. Build the battle you want.[/b]

Pick a country, coalition, or all-unit division. Field an enormous army, try a ready-made deck, or fight Zombie Horde. YSM gives Skirmish and Multiplayer battles more room to get out of hand.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Yokaiste/YSM/master/publish/headers/divisions.jpg[/img]

[h2]Unlimited[/h2]
999 units per card, with the same reserves at higher veterancy. Country, coalition, and all-unit divisions let you assemble combinations the standard rosters leave out. Great for experiments. Also great for discovering how many tanks your PC considers unreasonable.

[h2]Balanced[/h2]
Broad rosters with tighter reserves. Dynamic divisions use unit-specific availability drawn from standard divisions. Static divisions give every unit the same count at a given veterancy, with fewer at higher veterancy.

Balanced changes availability. Sandbox units remain available, so agree on house rules before somebody calls their nuclear bomber "close support."

Ready-made decks get you into a match quickly. The expanded armory and deck creator give you room to make your own.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Yokaiste/YSM/master/publish/headers/zombies.jpg[/img]

Pick the dedicated Zombie Horde division and field seven undead roles: Swarm, Breaker, Screecher, Stalker, Smoker, Pack Leader, and Demolisher. Or bring a conventional army and try to keep them out. Your front line is also their lunch.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Yokaiste/YSM/master/publish/headers/sandbox.jpg[/img]

Ground explosions can catch aircraft and helicopters in their blast radius.

A charcoal HUD and revised resource, selection, minimap, offmap, and chat panels help you manage the larger battles.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Yokaiste/YSM/master/publish/headers/compatibility.jpg[/img]

[url=https://github.com/dary1337/yuri-warno-toolkit]Yuri's WARNO Toolkit[/url] lets you combine YSM's features with a mod you already play. You can also add [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3387658237]WARNO Tactical Overhaul[/url] for different ranges, movement, and weapon behavior.

Compatibility depends on the mods you choose. Visit the [url=https://discord.gg/33Sqn6dTjf]YSM Community[/url] for help with combinations.
