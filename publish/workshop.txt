[h1]🧰 YSM × Any Mod — Through Yuri's WARNO Toolkit[/h1]

[b]Take a Workshop mod you already play, add YSM and generate one combined build in a few clicks.[/b]

[url=https://github.com/dary1337/yuri-warno-toolkit][b]Yuri's WARNO Toolkit (YWT)[/b][/url] is a desktop toolkit for decks, profiles, mod installation, and local combination workflows. Its [b]Builder[/b] tab uses custom integration logic powered by [url=https://github.com/Yokaiste/YMB][b]YMB[/b][/url] to assemble a Workshop mod with YSM and optional [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3387658237][b]WARNO Tactical Overhaul (WTO)[/b][/url].

[h2]🚀 Generate a Combination[/h2]
[olist]
[*][url=https://github.com/dary1337/yuri-warno-toolkit/releases/latest][b]Download the latest YWT release[/b][/url]—no installer or extra dependencies; Windows 10 or newer
[*]Subscribe to the base mod in the Steam Workshop
[*]Open YWT's [b]Builder[/b] tab and select that mod
[*]Choose [b]Add YSM[/b], optionally [b]Add WTO[/b], then press [b]Generate[/b]
[/olist]

[h2]🃏 Also Inside YWT[/h2]
[list]
[*]Edit and recover decks, including units, veterancy, transports, names, duplicates, and coalition switching
[*]Back up and restore profiles, or install YSM-family mods from the catalog
[/list]

[h2]📌 Good to Know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Combinations are generated entirely on your PC
[*]Compatibility varies. For help, send logs to the [url=https://discord.gg/J4wpAswAua][b]YSM Community[/b][/url]—not the base mod author
[*]Do not reupload or redistribute combined builds; every included mod remains the work of its original authors
[/list]

[h1]💡 Before You Launch[/h1]
[list]
[*]Subscribe, then [b]activate YSM in WARNO's Mod Center[/b]
[*]YSM is aimed at Skirmish and Multiplayer sandbox play
[*]Deploy only what you need if you would like your frame rate to remain involved in the battle
[*]Directly stacking gameplay or UI mods through WARNO will usually conflict; use YWT's Builder to generate a combined build
[*]Map mods are generally compatible
[/list]
